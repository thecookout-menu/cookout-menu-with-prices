What Makes the Cookout Menu Different

Most fast food places sell combo meals. Cookout does something a little different. It sells Trays. A Cookout Tray comes with one main item two side items and one drink. You can mix and match many items. The chain says there are over 54000 possible tray combinations. That means you can eat there many times and never order the same meal twice.

This tray system is the heart of the Cookout menu. It is also why the prices feel so low. Instead of paying extra for each side you get two sides included. You can pick things like hushpuppies Cajun fries onion rings or even a corn dog as a side. For many people this is the main reason to choose Cookout over other burger places.

How Much Does a Cookout Tray Cost in 2026

The tray is the best deal on the menu. Prices can be a little different depending on where you live but the general range is clear.

Regular trays usually cost between $7.39 and $12.49. The price depends on what main item you choose. A simple hamburger tray costs less. A tray with a Big Double burger or chicken strips costs more. Junior trays which are smaller cost between $6.39 and $9.99.

One of the best tricks at Cookout is the milkshake upgrade. You can trade your tray drink for a regular milkshake for just $1.00 more. For $1.60 more you can get a Fancy milkshake. This turns a cheap meal into a meal with dessert for very little extra money.

Burgers on the Cookout Menu

Burgers are a big part of the Cookout menu. All burgers are char grilled which gives them a smoky taste. The prices for just the burger without the tray are low.

The Small Burger is the cheapest option. It costs about $2.49 and has around 240 calories. The Big Double Burger is the most popular choice for hungry people. It has two patties and costs around $3.99 to $4.99 depending on the location. It has about 520 calories.

You can also order specialty styles. The Cook Out Style Burger comes with mustard ketchup chili and slaw. The Out West Style adds BBQ sauce and onion rings. These styles usually cost the same as a plain burger or just a little more. If you want a burger without the bun you can ask for a lettuce wrap. This is helpful for people who are trying to eat fewer carbs.

BBQ Hot Dogs and Chicken

Cookout is from the South so BBQ is important. The BBQ Sandwich is made with chopped pork and costs about $4.99. If you want more food the BBQ Plate costs around $5.99 and comes with a larger portion of meat. The meat is usually smoked and then chopped fine which is a style that comes from eastern North Carolina.

Hot dogs are another cheap favorite. A plain hot dog can cost as little as $2.49 or less at some locations. The Corn Dog and Chili Dog are also very cheap often around $1.99. One popular secret item is the Firecracker Hot Dog. You order a plain hot dog and ask for jalapenos chili and cheese sauce on top. It costs the same as a regular hot dog because the toppings are free.

For chicken you can get a Char Grilled Chicken Breast for about $4.85. It comes in styles like Original Cajun or BBQ. The Chicken Strip Club is a bigger meal. It costs about $5.99 and includes chicken strips bacon and cheese. If you want something lighter the Chicken Strip Snack is around $4.99.

Sides and Drinks

The sides at Cookout are a big part of the value. Because you get two sides with every tray you can try many different things. Hushpuppies are a Southern favorite. They are small fried cornmeal balls. A dozen hushpuppies cost about $1.99 if you order them alone. Cajun Fries are another popular choice. They are crispy and have a spicy seasoning. They cost about $1.99 as a side order.

Other side options include regular fries onion rings coleslaw and corn on the cob. You can even choose a corn dog or chicken nuggets as one of your two sides in a tray. This is a smart way to get more protein without paying more.

Drinks are simple. A regular soda or fresh brewed tea costs about $2.19 for a regular size. But as mentioned earlier the best deal is to upgrade to a milkshake for $1.00 more.

The Famous Milkshakes

Cookout is famous for its milkshakes. The chain offers over 40 flavors all year long. A regular milkshake costs $3.99. Some of the most popular flavors are Reeses Cup Oreo Mint Banana Pudding and Peanut Butter Banana.

The Reeses Cup Shake has about 894 calories so it is a very rich treat. If you want to try something unique you can ask the worker to blend two flavors together. Fans of the chain often mix Reeses Cup with Banana Fudge or Oreo Mint with Double Chocolate. This is not on the official menu but many locations will do it if you ask.

How Cookout Prices Compare to Other Chains

Research and statistics: A 2026 price comparison looked at Cookout Sonic Dairy Queen and Whataburger. The results show that Cookout is much cheaper for a full meal.

A signature combo meal at Sonic cost about $8.49. At Dairy Queen a similar basket was $8.99. At Whataburger a combo was $9.99. But a Cookout Tray started at just $5.49 for a basic burger or hot dog tray. Even a bigger tray with a Big Double burger was only about $6.99.

The milkshake price is also lower. A standard milkshake at Cookout was $3.59 in the comparison while Sonic charged $4.49 and Dairy Queen charged $5.49 for a Blizzard. Cookout keeps prices low because it is mostly company owned not franchised. That means the company can set prices without each owner adding extra costs. Tips to Save Money at Cookout

Here are a few simple ways to get the best value from the Cookout menu with prices:

  1. Always order a tray. Buying a burger two sides and a drink separately costs $3 to $5 more than getting the tray.
  2. Upgrade to a milkshake. For just $1.00 more you get a drink and a dessert in one. This is one of the best deals in fast food.
  3. Pick smart sides. You can choose a corn dog or chicken nuggets as a side. This adds more food to your meal without raising the price.
  4. Ask about student discounts. Some Cookout locations near colleges give discounts to students. You just need to ask at the window.
  5. Check the app. Cookout sometimes has app only deals or promotions for specific locations.
